Before you travel

  • Check current road conditions, weather and transport availability.
  • Confirm the exact destination and route with a local contact.
  • Ask permission before entering tea estate property or other private land.
  • Ask before photographing people, homes, workers or community activities.
  • Carry waste back with you and respect local instructions.

When to explore

For outdoor photography, early morning and late afternoon often provide softer natural light. Weather, season and local conditions affect the best time on a particular day.

Important visitor information

Distances, transport schedules, public access, accommodation, facilities and opening hours have not been independently verified on this website. Confirm these details locally before making a special trip. Do not assume a working tea estate is open to the public.
