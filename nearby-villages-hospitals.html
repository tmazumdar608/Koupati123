Koupati-area villages in government records

The Assam State Disaster Management Authority's Udalguri district plan for 2025–26 separately lists Khajuabil and Koupati Bagicha in its vulnerable-village records. These are administrative planning entries, not a statement that all neighbouring settlements share one village boundary or that any particular household is currently affected by a disaster.

Assam's Panchayat and Rural Development department also lists Koupati in the Rowta development-block context. Names and spellings can differ between public documents and local usage, so visitors should confirm precise village boundaries and directions with the relevant authorities.
