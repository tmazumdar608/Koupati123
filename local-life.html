Everyday life in Koupati

A destination is also its people, routines, local gathering places and weekly rhythms. This page introduces the community side of Koupati.

Temporary representative community-space photograph from the current collection. It is not confirmed as a photograph of Koupati Sunday Market.

The Sunday market

Koupati is known locally for a weekly Sunday market. Such markets are important gathering places where residents may meet, shop and exchange everyday goods. The exact range of products, operating hours and seasonal arrangements can change, so check with local residents before planning a visit.

Community and village surroundings

Local institutions, neighbourhoods, public spaces and familiar meeting points help shape everyday community life. When exploring, be considerate of residents and avoid photographing people, homes or private activities without permission.
