Koupati · Udalguri · Assam

A local introduction to Koupati

Koupati is a locality in Darrang district, Assam. This independent guide brings together introductory information, photographs and local stories to help people understand the area before visiting.

Temporary representative photograph from the current website collection. The exact location has not been independently verified as Koupati.

A place shaped by its surroundings

Koupati’s identity is connected to the wider Udalguri–Darrang region and Assam’s tea-growing landscape. Green surroundings, village routines and nearby community spaces are part of the everyday character of the area.

This website is an independent local information project. It is not a government tourism portal, an estate website or a substitute for local advice. Information is published to help people discover the area respectfully.

What you can explore

Use the guides on this website to learn about the tea-garden surroundings, local life, Assamese culture and practical travel considerations. Details such as opening hours, public access and transport availability should be confirmed locally before making a special trip.
